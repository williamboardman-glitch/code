TEMPLE OF BONES

A sunken temple, cracked open after a thousand years — and the dead inside never stopped guarding it. Run, jump, and shoot your way to the altar. Every zombie you drop leaves a soul behind, and a soul loaded in your gun is not the same as a bullet.

Shambler — fodder, no soul
Brute — soul loads a heavy 3x-damage shot
Fire Cultist — soul loads an explosive fire shot
Frost Priest — soul loads a freezing blast shot
Move: ← → / A D
Jump: Space / W (hold to jump higher)
Shoot: X / J (aim ↑ ↓ while holding)
Ammo: Keys 1-4
