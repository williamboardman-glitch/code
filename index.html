TEMPLE OF BONES

A sunken temple, cracked open after a thousand years — and the dead inside never stopped guarding it. Run, jump, and shoot your way through four floors — temple, dungeon, jungle, and finally Hell itself — to reach whatever's waiting at the bottom. You start with 100 bullets; run dry and you're down to a knife until your next kill — any kill, even a shambler — restocks two more. Every zombie you drop leaves a soul behind, and a soul loaded in your gun is not the same as a bullet. Floor two's exit is sealed behind the Guardian — walk into its arena and the way back shuts until one of you falls. Beat it, and you claim its shadow helm, which also unlocks Wither: a once-per-floor curse that halves a target's remaining health and the damage it deals. A wolf pup follows you from the start and grows fiercer with every kill, fully grown into Fenrir after 300. Hell belongs to one thing alone: the Arch Demon commands the entire floor, with no lesser zombies to hide behind. Between floors, spend what you've looted at the supply cache — though it charges more the deeper you go.
