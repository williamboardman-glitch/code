NECROMANCER'S NEST

You are the last necromancer of the Ashen Vale. From your tower you pick off the marching dead with a bone-forged rifle — but every kill leaves a soul behind. Harvest it, load it, and turn your enemies into your ammunition.

Skeleton Grunt — fodder, no soul
Berserker — soul loads a devastating heavy shot
Pyromancer — soul loads an explosive fire shot
Frost Witch — soul loads a freezing crowd-control shot
Aim: Mouse
Fire: Left Click (headshots one-shot kill)
Scope (bullet-time): Hold Right Click
Ammo: Keys 1-4
